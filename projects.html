Projects

Websites and apps I build and ship.

Each of these is online today. I develop websites and mobile apps, and I also administer some of them.

  • MaxiPlein

    Fuel price comparison for France. It lists Diesel, SP95, SP98, E10, E85 and LPG prices at service stations across the country and shows EV charging points, using official data refreshed every 10 minutes. A Next.js website with a companion Expo mobile app.

    maxiplein.com

  • MaxiPlein app

    The mobile version of MaxiPlein. It finds the cheapest fuel around you, on a list or a map, with prices per fuel, station details and directions. Built with React Native and Expo, available on the App Store and Google Play.

    Google Play

  • Food Loot

    The website of Food Loot, a personal map of places to eat: find restaurants near you, photograph the dishes you loved and keep your reviews. It presents the app and links to its store page.

    foodloot.app

  • Food Loot app

    The Food Loot mobile app. Find restaurants near you, photograph the dishes you loved, keep your reviews and lists, and share them with friends. Built with Expo and TypeScript, backed by a Django and PostGIS API. Available on Google Play.

    Google Play

  • HandyMake

    A DIY tutorials and projects website with step-by-step guides for making, building and cooking. Built with Django.

    handymake.com

  • France Patinoire

    A directory and news site for ice rinks across France, with rink listings and ticketing information.

    francepatinoire.fr

  • Billettera Organizer

    The mobile app for organizers of Billettera, an online ticketing platform. From their phone, organizers create a sales page, set up a ticketing with its tickets and promo codes, follow their sales and put the event on sale. Built with React Native and Expo for iOS and Android. I contributed to its design and to the app.

    billettera.com

  • YD Création

    A showcase website for an art blacksmith based in Mauguio, France, presenting his made-to-measure ironwork: wall lights, gates, light fixtures, range hoods, signs, furniture and railings.

    ydcreation.fr
