About

I’m Olivier Paul Collorafi. I build websites and mobile apps from Nice, France.

I run OC Consulting, a sole proprietorship registered in France since December 2018. I design, build and ship websites and mobile apps for clients, and I advise teams on technical decisions such as architecture and technology choices.

I also build and run my own products. MaxiPlein compares fuel prices across France, Food Loot is a companion app for the meals you want to remember, and HandyMake publishes DIY tutorials. For clients, I built France Patinoire, a directory of ice rinks, the organizer mobile app of Billettera, an online ticketing platform, and a showcase website for an art blacksmith. Looking after products end to end keeps my advice practical.

I work remotely and talk with clients by email and video call. If you have a project in mind or want a second technical opinion, send me an email.
